# School and student council

I have been at **Sekolah Bogor Raya** since kindergarten. I like being the person who connects what students think with what the school does.

## What I have done

- **Junior High School Student Council**, 2025-2026. Social and Student Services Unit. I helped organise SBR Run and SMP Forum OSIS, was the MC for the General Assembly, and served as a flag officer in the flag raising ceremony.
- **Class Secretary, 7B**, 2025-2026. I kept the class records and schedules in order and passed messages between students and teachers.
- **Primary Student Council**, 2024-2025. Environment Unit, and one of the candidates for President. The interview and the campaign taught me public speaking.
