# For the planet

When I was in Grade 1 to Grade 3, I was obsessed with **Jane Goodall**, who dedicated her life to animal research. I drew myself with woolly monkeys in the Amazon rainforest and wrote a short essay about my dream. It was selected for [*Ich habe einen Traum - I have a dream*](https://www.aufbau-verlage.de/aufbau-digital/ich-habe-einen-traum-i-have-dream/978-3-8412-2634-1), a book of writing by children from around the world, published with the German Federal Foreign Office in 2020.

My pages are 280 to 283.

## Mema

In Grade 4, experts from the Borneo Orangutan Survival (BOS) Foundation visited my school. I joined the question session and won a baby orangutan doll, which I still keep. After that I asked my parents if we could adopt an orangutan.

Since then I have been a mama to an orangutan called **Mema**. [See how cute she is](https://www.orangutan.or.id/mema). BOS also plants a tree in my name every time we renew the adoption.

> Even if it is small, everybody needs to contribute for a better future of our planet.
