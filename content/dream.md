# The dream

My goal is to go to university in **Germany** and study either **science** or **art history**. I am especially interested in Heidelberg. I became curious about the Middle Ages when my parents took me to museums in Munich in 2019, and I want to visit more historic places while I study.

I am serious about it, so I am already learning German. Right now I am at level **A2**.

## Who I look up to

My role model changes whenever my interests change. First it was Jane Goodall. Now it is **Marie Curie**. I admire her for her discoveries, and also because she had a tough childhood and still became the best she could be. She was the first woman to win two Nobel Prizes.

> I hope someday my picture is listed among women in science as well!
