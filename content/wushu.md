# Wushu

Wushu is my sport. I compete in **taolu**, which means routines: you perform a set of moves, with or without a weapon, and the judges score it. I also practise **Wuzuquan**, a traditional kung fu style. I am currently listed as a **Bogor City PELATDA athlete**.

> I can do up to 400 frog jumps in one go.

## What I perform

- **Changquan** - Long Fist
- **Daoshu** - Broadsword routine
- **Gunshu** - Cudgel routine
- **Wuzuquan** - Five Ancestors Fist, traditional kung fu
